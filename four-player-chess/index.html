THE RULEBOOK

Four sides. New tactics.

Familiar chess, with more to consider.

The board

Four 16-piece armies face one another across a 14 × 14 board with its four corners removed. The turn order is Red → Blue → Yellow → Green.

Moving & attacking

Pieces move as in ordinary chess, oriented toward each player's opposing edge. Pawns promote on the far side. Checks, castling and en passant are enforced.

Victory

If a player has no legal moves, that color is eliminated and its pieces leave the board. The last active color wins in free-for-all, or the last active team wins in team mode.

Teams

Red and Yellow cooperate against Blue and Green. Teammates cannot capture each other's pieces.

Variant differences

This is Quadra's own documented ruleset, not an exact recreation of Chess.com's four-player scoring and elimination format.

Want a future format? The shared rules engine is being structured for other chess variants and games.
