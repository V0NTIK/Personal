Rules & game format

Board: 14 × 14 with the four 3 × 3 corners removed. Each player begins with eight back-rank pieces and eight pawns.

Moves: Standard chess piece movement rotated to each army's home edge. Pawns promote at the far edge. Castling and en passant are supported.

Elimination: A player with no legal move is eliminated. Their pieces are removed from play. Last remaining player (or team) wins.

Teams: Teammates cannot capture each other. Your king must be safe from enemy attacks; teammates share victory.

Variant note: This is the site's clearly defined local ruleset, not a claim of exact compatibility with Chess.com's official four-player scoring and dead-piece rules.
